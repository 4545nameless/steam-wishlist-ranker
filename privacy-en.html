Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026  |  日本語

Steam Wishlist Ranker (the "Site") handles your information as described below.

1. Information you enter and how it is handled

To load your wishlist, the Site asks you to enter your SteamID64. Your SteamID64, together with your ranking progress, results, and settings such as your chosen language, is stored only in your own browser (localStorage). The operator does not run a database to collect or store users' information.

When your wishlist is loaded, the SteamID64 you entered is sent to Steam through a relay server (Cloudflare Workers) that forwards requests to the Steam API. This relay is used only to complete the request. Logging on the relay server is disabled, and the operator does not store, record, or use this information. If a Steam Web API key was entered in an earlier version of the Site and is still saved, it is removed from your browser when the Site starts.

To show price history such as the lowest price on Steam, the App IDs (game numbers) of the games in your wishlist are sent through the relay server (Cloudflare Workers) to the IsThereAnyDeal API. Your SteamID64 is not sent to IsThereAnyDeal.

2. Applying your ranking to Steam (bookmarklet)

To apply your ranking to your Steam wishlist, you save a bookmark whose code contains your ranking (the ordered list of game IDs) in your own browser. Only when you click that bookmark on your Steam wishlist page is the order sent to Steam. This happens entirely in your browser, and nothing is sent to the operator's servers.

3. Advertising

The Site may use Google AdSense, an advertising service provided by Google, a third-party vendor. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this Site or other sites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Donations

The Site has an optional donation link (Ko-fi) to support its operation. Payments and the handling of your information in the donation process are governed by the policies of Ko-fi and its payment providers.

5. Third-party services

The Site uses html2canvas (loaded via unpkg) to generate images, Sortable.js (loaded via jsDelivr) to reorder rankings, Vercel Analytics to understand site traffic, various Steam services to retrieve game information and images, and the IsThereAnyDeal API to show historical low prices. Vercel Analytics collects aggregate traffic statistics without using cookies.

6. Disclaimer

The Site is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with Valve Corporation or Steam. The operator is not liable for any damages arising from the use of the Site.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as needed. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.

8. Contact
