Changelog

The main updates to Steam Wishlist Ranker, newest first. Small fixes are left out.

October 2026

October 31

  • Moved to a new address (steam-wishlist-ranker.pages.dev). Rankings are saved in your browser separately for each address, so rankings made at the old address can't be carried over. Please load your wishlist again.
  • Added a how-to guide, an FAQ, five articles, an About page and Terms of Use (in Japanese and English).

October 28

  • You can now start by pasting your wishlist or profile URL. You no longer need to look up your SteamID64 yourself.

October 27

  • You can now add your result to your Steam wishlist as categories (🔥 Want it now / 🏷️ Buy on sale / 💭 Maybe someday / 🗑️ Probably skip). See the article "Use Steam wishlist categories to catch the right sales" for how to use them.
  • When you load your wishlist again, your previous ranking is kept and only newly added games are slotted in.
  • The tier names on the result screen now match the category names.
  • Added links to follow on X and to contact us.
  • Fixed: share image layout glitches, missing spaces in English text, and the middle and lower ranks getting mixed up when you ran another round on the top games.

October 26

  • Game details are now fetched in batches, which makes loading much faster. A 274-game wishlist went from tens of seconds to a few seconds.
  • Release dates of upcoming games now look the same as on the Steam store (year only, month, quarter and so on).

October 25

  • The page now scrolls while you drag a game on the result screen.

October 24

  • Rebuilt how ranking works. You now narrow things down step by step: tap the games that catch your eye, pick from 4, then 1-on-1 for the top games and a final bracket. "Raid battles," where lower-ranked games challenge the top, were added too. The how-to guide and the article "How the ranking works without comparing everything" explain it in detail.
  • Added two finishing rounds after the final: "Middle sort" and "Weakest showdown," which finds the game you want least.
  • A Steam Web API key is no longer needed.
  • You can now apply your order to your Steam wishlist with a single bookmark click. Steps for phones are included.
  • The all-time low price on Steam (data from IsThereAnyDeal) is now shown.
  • Store prices are now shown in your country's currency.
  • The result is now split into four tiers, and you can fine-tune the order by dragging. Keyboard controls work too.
  • Redesigned the share post text and image.
  • Added a logo.
  • Fixed the result table getting cut off on phones.

October 22

  • Added picking the most and least wanted game out of 4. It's much faster than comparing games 1-on-1 alone.
  • Added effects such as confetti when you make a pick.

October 21

  • Added support for 6 languages: Japanese, English, Chinese, Russian, Portuguese and German.
  • Published the privacy policy.
  • Added step-by-step help for finding your SteamID64 and for applying your order to Steam.
  • Fixed copying the share image when posting to X.

October 20

  • Launched Steam Wishlist Ranker. The first version built a ranking by comparing the games on your wishlist 1-on-1.
